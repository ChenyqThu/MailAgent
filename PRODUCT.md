# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MailAgent serves a single power user who owns the local SQLite source of truth and manages a high-volume professional email workflow across Mail.app or DavMail, Notion, calendar, KOS, and AI agents. The user works primarily in the macOS desktop app, with remote web access as a secondary surface, and needs fast keyboard-driven triage, trustworthy automation, and clear recovery when background work fails or pauses for approval.

## Product Purpose

MailAgent is a local-first command center for email and adjacent knowledge work. It synchronizes and enriches mail, exposes deterministic search and operational controls, and lets AI agents classify, research, draft, schedule, and produce durable reports without hiding provenance, permissions, or execution state. Success means the user can delegate substantial work while retaining an auditable path back to the source email, the exact run, and every consequential action.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, and quietly capable. The product should feel like a native professional instrument: dense without being cramped, intelligent without performing intelligence, and distinctive through disciplined details rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards built from interchangeable feature-card grids.
- Purple-gradient "AI productivity" products and decorative AI branding.
- Mobile layouts enlarged for desktop, especially oversized controls and over-rounded cards.
- Chat-first interfaces that obscure the underlying mail, report, or operational workflow.
- Automation UIs that present partial, paused, skipped, or failed work as successful.
- Decorative glass, glow, motion, or color that competes with information and state.

## Design Principles

1. Make trust observable: expose provenance, permissions, run state, approvals, costs, and failure causes at the point of action.
2. Treat information density as a capability: prefer rows, grouped activity, progressive disclosure, and compact controls over card sprawl.
3. Keep AI subordinate to the job: agents should organize and complete mail work, not turn every surface into a chat product.
4. Preserve native desktop rhythm: keyboard and mouse are peers, controls are familiar, and motion communicates state rather than spectacle.
5. Use one source of truth across layers: mirrored schemas, enums, schedules, and visual meanings require shared definitions or automated consistency gates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for supported desktop and web surfaces. Maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text, preserve visible keyboard focus and complete keyboard operation, avoid color-only state communication, support reduced motion, and keep Chinese UI text at 14px or larger. All new user-facing copy must ship in both zh-CN and en-US through the existing i18n system.
